# product-to-pixels

Purpose: convert product evidence into an implementation-ready Design Contract.

Inputs: Product Opportunity Brief, Evidence Pack, platform, mode, timebox, UX references.

Outputs: promise, hero moment, experience spine, core flow, screens/states, hierarchy, UX findings, visual brief, exactly three actionable design principles, tokens, signature component, motion, accessibility, platform mapping, cut list, acceptance criteria.

Gate: promise, hero moment, primary flow, screen scope, and exactly three actionable principles are defined.

Handoff: `mvp-forge`.
