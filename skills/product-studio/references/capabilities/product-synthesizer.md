# product-synthesizer

Purpose: combine original idea, evidence, and MVP findings into an Updated Product Definition.

Outputs: target segment, problem, validated value proposition, core loop, proven/promising/demo-only/removed features, monetization, retention, distribution, data strategy, competitive position, metrics, next experiment, production-readiness verdict.

Gate: validated and unproven claims are separated; production is not treated as a cleaned-up MVP.

Handoff: `production-blueprint` or next experiment.
